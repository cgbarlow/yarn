# Principles and primitives

## First principles

1. **The human is the active ingredient.** The AI amplifies someone who loves the child. It never substitutes for them.
2. **It initiates.** Help-seeking is the bottleneck, so the system opens the conversation.
3. **The lesson is the plot.** The skill is the only way the story moves. No points sprinkled on worksheets.
4. **Failure is the curriculum.** It never hands over answers, and it makes failing survivable and interesting.
5. **Success means being needed less.** The goal is self-reliance, so measure independence, not engagement.
6. **One child, many years, family-owned.** The record belongs to the family and runs local-first.
7. **It points outward.** Towards real people, peers and the physical world. Each child's path diverges, which is the opposite of the Mouse Army.
8. **A book, not a being.** No persona, no "I", no simulated affection. The relationships are with people. The system is the book they share.

## Primitives

| Primitive | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ractor channel** | Routes the day's struggle to a parent, grandparent or mentor with one good question to ask. Their voice carries the important moments. |
| **Learner model** | A persistent, portable record of what the child knows, loves and avoids. |
| **Story engine** | A long-running personal world with saved state, where skill gates are plot gates. |
| **Verified challenges** | Problems checked by code, not by the model's opinion. |
| **Initiative scheduler** | Decides when to speak, what to open with and when to stop. |
| **Struggle governor** | A hint ladder that tells productive struggle from the hopeless kind. |
| **Fade meter** | Withdraws support as competence grows and reports it. |
| **World bridge** | Quests that leave the screen: build it, ask Grandad, teach your sister. |
| **Open ledger** | Adults can see everything. Session caps. No dark patterns. |

## Why it is new

Current products are help desks (Khanmigo) or engagement machines (companion apps and AI toys). Three things here are new:

- AI that directs human attention to the child, instead of replacing it
- Narrative as a multi-year curriculum
- A product designed to make itself unnecessary

## Pragmatic start

Voice-first, ages 5 to 9, reading and maths, a handful of families, one year. All of it is buildable with today's models, memory and speech.
