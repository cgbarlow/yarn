# Open questions

Things the thinking so far has not settled.

## To prove

1. **Does the story lever work?** Do children come back, night after night, for a story gated on skills?
2. **Do skills transfer?** Do they hold on paper, cold, with no story and no hints?
3. **Does the human channel raise time together?** Or do parents outsource to it?
4. **Does engagement produce learning at all?** No AI tutor study has shown it yet.

## To decide

1. **Form.** Tablet and phone first, then a story box or e-ink book?
2. **Who fills the empty chair?** How the system finds another human when a parent has no time.
3. **Business model.** A fixed-term price that pays for graduation, not retention.
4. **How the ledger loosens with age.** Full view at six. What at twelve?
5. **Content.** Who writes the skill graph and story scaffolds, and against which curriculum?
6. **Where it runs.** How much can sit on a box at home with current hardware.
7. **Name clearance.** Trademarks and domains.

## Suggested first step

A proper landscape review, then a small trial: a handful of families, ages 5 to 9, reading and maths, one year, with the cold paper check as the main measure.
